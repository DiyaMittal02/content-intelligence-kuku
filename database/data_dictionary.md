## Metric definitions (use these exact definitions in the case study)

| Metric | Definition (numerator / denominator / window) |
|---|---|
| **Active user (DAU)** | Distinct users with ≥1 consumption event of ≥30s on that calendar day (IST) |
| **Stickiness** | Avg DAU over 28 days ÷ MAU for the same 28 days |
| **D7 retention** | Users in a signup cohort active on day 7 (state whether exact-day or days 7–13) ÷ cohort size |
| **Activation** | New users completing ≥3 episodes within 72h of signup ÷ new users |
| **Episode completion** | Plays reaching ≥90% of duration ÷ plays started |
| **Paywall conversion** | Users who subscribe within 24h of first paywall_view ÷ users with a paywall_view |
| **Trial-to-paid** | Trials converting to a first paid charge ÷ trials that ended in the period |
| **Renewal rate** | Subscriptions renewed ÷ subscriptions due for renewal in the period |
| **ARPPU** | Revenue in period ÷ paying users in period |
| **Payment failure rate** | Failed payment attempts ÷ total payment attempts (segment by method) |

## Planted patterns (the generator bakes these in on purpose)

| # | Pattern | Exact mechanism | How to detect |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **High-hook / low-retention genre** | Per-genre retention curve `base·exp(-day/tau)`: Thriller (0.80, τ=4) & Horror (0.74, τ=5) decay fast; Drama (0.50, τ=23) & Romance (0.52, τ=20) are sticky. Users preferentially watch titles in their driving genre. | `04_retention_cohorts.sql` grouped by genre of first watched title → Thriller tops D1, bottoms D30. |
| 2 | **UPI payment-failure spike** | During **2026-08-10 → 2026-08-16**, UPI failure prob = 0.40 vs 0.06 baseline. | `07_monetisation.sql` failure rate by ISO week × method → week of 2026-08-11 ≈ 36%. |
| 3 | **Paywall position effect** | Micro-dramas split `paywall_ep6` vs `paywall_ep8`; subscribe prob rises with episodes watched before the wall, so ep8 (2 more free episodes) converts higher. | `09_experiment_readout.sql` → ep8 subscribe-rate ≈ +4–5pp over ep6. |

Validate at any time with `python database/validate_patterns.py`.
